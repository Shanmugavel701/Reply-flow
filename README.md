1. PRODUCT VISION

Build a production-quality SaaS platform for businesses, ecommerce stores, creators, agencies, and service businesses that automates customer conversations and sales across:

Instagram

WhatsApp

Website

WooCommerce

Email

CRM

AI-powered customer conversations

The core promise of the product is:

"Your business keeps talking to customers, even while you sleep."

The platform should help businesses:

Automatically reply to Instagram comments

Automatically send Instagram DMs

Automate Instagram story replies

Automate Instagram conversations

Automate WhatsApp customer communication

Capture leads from Instagram and websites

Recover abandoned carts

Follow up with leads

Send order updates

Request reviews

Bring previous customers back

Recommend products

Create automated marketing campaigns

Track revenue generated by automation

This is NOT just an Instagram automation tool.

Position it as:

A Business Conversation & Sales Automation Platform.
